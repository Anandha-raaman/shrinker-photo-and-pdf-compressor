Frequently Asked Questions Before Reaching Out

Q: Why is my file size slightly different on my computer vs upload portal?
A: Windows/macOS and server operating systems calculate file sizes in Kibibytes (KiB = 1024 bytes) vs standard KB (1000 bytes). Shrinker targets strict binary byte limits to ensure universal compliance across all portal validators.

Q: Are files stored on your servers?
A: No. All files are processed 100% locally in your browser memory. We never receive or store any user files.
