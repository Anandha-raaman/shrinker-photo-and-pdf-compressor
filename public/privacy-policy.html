Privacy Policy for Shrinkr: PDF & Image Compressor

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Shrinkr. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your documents remain completely confidential.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

Shrinkr operates entirely on your local device. When you select, compress, or view photos or PDF files, all processing takes place locally within your device's memory. None of your photos, documents, or personal files are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external servers or cloud services.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

3. Zero Tracking and Zero Analytics

Shrinkr does not include any third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

4. App Permissions

The app only requests standard file access permissions required to allow you to select and save your compressed images and PDF documents to your device. These files are processed ephemerally and remain solely on your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@shrinkr.app.
