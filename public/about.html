About Shrinker — Photo & PDF Compressor

Empowering students, job applicants, and professionals with secure, on-device document compression.

Our Core Mission: Shrinker was created to solve a widespread problem faced by millions of competitive exam candidates, job seekers, and visa applicants worldwide: rigid file size limits (such as 20KB, 50KB, 100KB) on official portals, paired with privacy concerns surrounding uploading personal documents to remote servers.

Who We Are

Shrinker is a modern, high-performance web utility built for speed, accuracy, and absolute user privacy. Whether you are applying for UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET, college admissions, passport services, or international visas, Shrinker provides exact file size targeting without compromising document clarity.

Why We Are Different: 100% Client-Side Technology

Unlike traditional online compression websites that transmit your sensitive photographs, signatures, identity cards, and marksheets across the Internet to third-party cloud servers, Shrinker operates entirely within your web browser.

🔒 Total Privacy

Your files are processed 100% locally in your device's memory using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Zero files are uploaded or stored on any server.

🎯 Exact KB Targeting

Our intelligent binary-search algorithm scales image dimensions and quantization tables to hit strict KB thresholds (e.g. 20KB or 50KB) on your first try.

⚡ Offline & Instant

Because there are no server round-trips or network bottlenecks, file compression completes in milliseconds—even when working offline.

Supported Use Cases & Examinations

  • UPSC Civil Services / NDA / CDS: Compress signatures to 10–20 KB and passport photos to 20–50 KB.
  • SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD): Resize candidate photos to 20–50 KB and signature scans to 10–20 KB.
  • Banking Exams (IBPS PO, Clerk, SBI): Format photos, signatures, thumb impressions, and handwritten declarations.
  • NTA (NEET-UG, JEE Main, CUET): Resize passport photos and multi-page PDF certificates under 100 KB and 200 KB.
  • International Visas & Passports: Standardize passport photographs for Schengen, US DS-160, and national passport portals.

Open Source & Technical Transparency

Shrinker is built using React, TypeScript, standard Web APIs, and client-side canvas libraries. We are committed to maintaining a clean, ad-supported free model so candidates can access essential tools without paying subscriptions or surrendering personal data.
