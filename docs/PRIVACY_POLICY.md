# Privacy Policy for Shrinkr: PDF & Image Compressor

**Effective Date:** September 2026

At **Shrinkr**, user privacy and data security are our top priorities. This document outlines how our application operates and handles data.

---

### 1. 100% On-Device Local Processing
Shrinkr operates completely offline and on-device. When you select an image or PDF file to compress, resize, or convert:
- All processing algorithms execute directly within your device's memory.
- **No files are ever transmitted, uploaded, or cached on external cloud servers.**

### 2. Zero Data Collection
Shrinkr does not collect, record, or track:
- Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
- Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location coordinates.
- Document contents or file metadata.

### 3. No Advertising SDKs or Third-Party Trackers
The application contains zero advertising SDKs, analytic pixels, or background telemetry.

### 4. Permissions Required
- **Storage / Media Access**: Required strictly to let you pick images or PDFs from your device and save the compressed outputs back to your device storage.

### 5. Google Play Data Safety Compliance
- **Data Collected**: None
- **Data Shared**: None
- **Data Encrypted in Transit**: Not applicable (zero data transmitted)
- **Account Requirement**: None
